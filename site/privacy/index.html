Privacy

This covers the hosted beta at app.tripcord.dev. If you self-host Tripcord, your data stays on your server and none of this applies.

What's stored

  • Your account: your email, your name, a hash of your password, and your GitHub user id if you connect GitHub.
  • Timelines: whatever your app records, plus the page URL (origin and path by default) and the browser's user agent.
  • No IP addresses: none are stored, with timelines or in the server's logs. The server uses them briefly, in memory, to rate-limit requests.

Where

On a server at OVH, in France. Encrypted backups are stored with Backblaze (B2), in their EU data center in Amsterdam. Nothing leaves the EU.

Who processes it

  • OVH runs the server and sends our email.
  • Backblaze stores the encrypted backups.
  • GitHub hosts this website, and handles GitHub login if you use it.

How long

Timelines are kept for 30 days. Accounts are kept until you delete them. Backups are kept for 14 days. When you delete something, it's gone from the database immediately, and from backups within 14 days.

What we never do

We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or track visitors. This site has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts.

Contact

Write to hello@tripcord.dev, including if you need a data processing agreement.
